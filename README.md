One image summing up the world's news of Saturday, 21 November 2026.
Entry for make.fun's "Sum up 21 November's world news in one image" bounty.

The front page leads with Russia's overnight barrage on Ukraine — more
than 400 drones and 110 missiles, at least 8 killed in Kyiv — and missiles
landing in Poland, then covers the day's other big stories: the AI stock
sell-off pushing the S&P 500 into correction, the International Court of
Justice finding Myanmar responsible for genocide against the Rohingya, the
Trump–Xi–Putin peace declaration after the Shenzhen summit, a pancreatic
cancer pill making tumours undetectable in trial, COP31 running past its
deadline, and Gen Z protests across 25 European countries.

`entry.png` is the image (1024×1024, JPEG bytes under the name entry.png,
as the bounty allows). `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies
it to `/output/entry.png`, so both graded runs write identical bytes.

## Run it

mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
